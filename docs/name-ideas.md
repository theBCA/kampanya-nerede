# Project Name Ideas

Recommended shortlist:

1. Fiyat Pusula
   - Broad, memorable, and works beyond simple coupons.
   - Good fit if the product becomes a price-intelligence platform.

2. Gercek Indirim
   - Very clear value proposition.
   - Strong for trust, but more descriptive than brand-like.

3. Sepet Akli
   - Points directly at the product's real edge: basket-level final price logic.
   - Better for a smart assistant feel than a deals directory.

4. Kampanya Radar
   - Easy to understand and search-friendly.
   - Slightly generic, but strong for the Turkish market.

5. Firsat Pusulasi
   - Consumer-friendly and broad.
   - Less technical, more shopping-oriented.

6. Net Fiyat
   - Short and direct.
   - Best if the main promise is final payable price after all discounts.

7. Indirim Nabzi
   - Good for trend and live-deal positioning.
   - More media/community feel than utility product.

8. Avantaj Haritasi
   - Works for campaigns, card benefits, and retailer advantages.
   - Slightly more formal.

9. Sepette Ne Var
   - Friendly and memorable.
   - Better for a consumer app than B2B analytics.

10. Firsat Dedektifi
    - Trust and investigation angle is strong.
    - More playful, less enterprise-friendly.

## My pick

Use `Fiyat Pusula` if you want the product to become bigger than coupons and campaigns.

Use `Gercek Indirim` if you want the first public message to be extremely clear: only real discounts, backed by price history.

For GitHub, `kampanya-nerede` is a safe working slug because it matches the existing project idea and can be renamed later without losing repo history.
